Our commitment

MediCore is designed to support coordinated care. These terms explain the responsibilities that help keep the workspace dependable, respectful, and appropriate for handling health information.

01

Using MediCore

MediCore may be used only by staff authorized by their healthcare organization. Use the workspace for legitimate clinical, administrative, scheduling, prescription, and billing activities within your assigned role.

Keep your account credentials private. Do not allow another person to use your account, bypass access controls, or use the service for unlawful, harmful, or unrelated purposes.

02

Privacy & patient information

Access only the patient information needed to perform your duties. Treat all records as confidential and follow the privacy, consent, retention, and disclosure requirements that apply to your organization.

Handle with careAvoid exporting, printing, photographing, or storing patient information outside approved systems unless your organization has authorized the activity.
03

Security responsibilities

Use a strong, unique password and sign out when you finish, especially on shared devices. Keep devices updated and report suspected unauthorized access, lost devices, or security concerns to your administrator without delay.

MediCore applies account and access protections, but every team member shares responsibility for maintaining a secure care environment.

04

Clinical records & billing

Enter information accurately and review it before finalizing an appointment, prescription, clinical note, invoice, payment, or balance. MediCore helps organize records; your organization remains responsible for clinical judgement, financial decisions, and regulatory compliance.

05

Service availability

We may maintain, update, or temporarily restrict access to protect security and reliability. Keep your organization’s local downtime and emergency procedures available for situations where access to the workspace is interrupted.

06

Questions & contact

For questions about these terms, privacy practices, or your organization’s use of MediCore, contact your administrator or reach the MediCore care team through our contact page.
